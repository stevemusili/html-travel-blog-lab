Adventures in Fire Island

My experience at Fire Island National Park was amazing. I was able to see alot of life in the park, from tiny diatoms and insects to fin whales and even a white-tailed deer.

I fully immersed myself with the enchanting collage of the rhythmic waves, wildlife, historic landmarks, high dunes and maritime forests.

Gallery: Fire Island

Explore the Adventure!

Visiting the Fire Island is worth it! The park includes everything from wildlife, camping, sea shore sites, history (where you get to visit the William Floyd Estate) and the rhythmic waves at the beach. You too should take a visit at the park!
